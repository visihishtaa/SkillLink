# SkillLink – Empowering Local Workforce

### Connecting Local Skills with Local Opportunities

SkillLink is a web-based platform designed to connect local skilled workers directly with individuals, households, and organizations looking for their services.

The platform aims to reduce the gap between skilled workers and employers by providing an accessible space for job discovery, worker profiles, communication, and service hiring.

SkillLink supports **SDG 8 – Decent Work and Economic Growth**, **SDG 10 – Reduced Inequalities**, and **SDG 1 – No Poverty**.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Many skilled workers, including electricians, plumbers, carpenters, mechanics, gardeners, and domestic helpers, struggle to find regular employment due to limited digital access, lack of professional networks, and dependency on middlemen.

At the same time, customers often face difficulties finding reliable workers nearby.

This gap creates challenges such as:

- Limited employment opportunities for local workers.
- Irregular income and financial instability.
- Dependency on intermediaries for finding jobs.
- Difficulty identifying suitable and trustworthy workers.
- Lack of a centralized platform for local service discovery.

## 💡 Our Solution

SkillLink provides a digital platform where skilled workers can showcase their services and connect directly with potential employers.

Through worker profiles, job postings, location-based discovery, and communication features, the platform aims to make local employment more accessible and transparent.

**Our goal is simple: Help skilled workers find opportunities and help employers find the right people.**

## ✨ Key Features

### 👷 Worker Profiles
Workers can create profiles highlighting their skills, experience, location, and services.

### 📍 Location-Based Worker Discovery
Employers can search for workers based on their location and service requirements.

### 💼 Job Posting
Employers can describe their requirements and connect with suitable workers.

### 💬 Direct Communication
Workers and employers can communicate to discuss job requirements and availability.

### ⭐ Ratings and Reviews
Feedback helps employers make informed hiring decisions and allows workers to build their professional reputation.

### 🌍 Multilingual Accessibility
Designed to support users from different linguistic backgrounds.

### 🤝 Direct Employment Opportunities
Reduces dependency on middlemen and encourages transparent communication between workers and employers.

## ⚙️ How SkillLink Works

**Step 1: Registration**

Workers and employers access the SkillLink platform.

**Step 2: Worker Profile Creation**

Workers provide their skills, experience, location, and service details.

**Step 3: Service Discovery**

Employers search for workers based on their requirements.

**Step 4: Worker Selection**

Employers review available worker profiles and identify suitable candidates.

**Step 5: Communication**

Workers and employers discuss the job requirements directly.

**Step 6: Service Completion and Feedback**

After completing the service, employers can provide ratings and reviews.
